Books for this trimester

    Daily readings

    Tick each part as you finish it. The day's main reading follows a fixed rotation: Monday and Wednesday philosophy, Tuesday and Thursday theology, Friday history.

    How to use the hours

    Latin, 1 hour. One Ørberg chapter a week. Add new words to Anki every day and do your reviews first.

    Scripture and Catechism, 30 minutes. Year One covers the Bible's narrative books and the Gospels. Wisdom, prophets and epistles follow in Year Two. The Catechism is read in full across the year.

    Main reading, 2 hours. Read the primary text, then write a short summary in your commonplace book. Stephanus and Bekker numbers are given so any translation works.

    Music, 30 minutes. Theory or chant from Monday to Thursday, then focused listening on Friday.

    On a 2-hour day, keep Latin and Scripture and halve the main reading. Move music to your commute. A missed day stays unticked, so catch it up in week 14.
